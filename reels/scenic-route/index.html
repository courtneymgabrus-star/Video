Nobody tells you your
path can look like
a scribble and still
make sense.
I grew up on
Long Island:
Bellmore,
Sea Cliff,
the North Shore.
I was always the
kid drawing outside the lines.
I started at community
college and earned my associate's.
Then I moved to
Tampa in 2015 to
finish my bachelor's.
Life had other plans,
and that's okay.
At 30 I moved
to Hilton Head with
my mom.
Starting over at 30
is scary.
It's also kind of magic.
Now I'm a graphic
designer and children's book author.
I run Court Creations
on Etsy.
I founded Life Outside
the Lines to make
books where every kid
sees themselves.
And I'm writing my memoir,
One in a Million.
So if your path
looks messy right now,
keep going.
The scenic route still
gets you there.
